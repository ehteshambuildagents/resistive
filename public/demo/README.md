# Sample asset provenance
These are illustrative demonstration assets, not a real brand, customer, campaign or Resistive output.

contact-sheet.png was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: Photorealistic five-panel product contact sheet; same unbranded ivory cylindrical device with charcoal collar and removable pale blue cartridge; closed product, hands twisting open, inserting cartridge, clicking closed, held ready; consistent silhouette, neutral tabletop setting, no branding or text. No skincare or fictional customer cues.

ugc-sheet.png was generated with OpenArt (GPT Image 2, text2image, 21:9, 1k/medium). Prompt: photographic contact sheet of five vertical user-generated-content testimonial ad frames, five different women aged 30-50 filmed selfie-style talking to camera at home, the same unbranded ivory cylindrical device with charcoal collar resting beside them or held but never operated, no text or logos. It represents the testimonial creative this illustrative brand has over-tested, and is deliberately the visual opposite of contact-sheet.png, where hands actually operate the product. The two reels together are what makes the 83-vs-4 imbalance visible rather than merely asserted.

formats-sheet.png was generated with OpenArt (Kling 3 Omni, text2image, 21:9, 1k) to widen the creative library beyond testimonials. QA note: only panels 1 (collar macro) and 3 (kitchen lifestyle) hold the established product identity; panels 2, 4 and 5 rendered a squat wide body with an oversized cartridge and are NOT used, because proportion consistency across the whole film matters more than filling tiles. The remaining format variety is produced by re-cropping the on-identity reels via the Photo zoom/pan props, which is how paid social genuinely varies one shot.

sample-ad.mp4 is a 15-second 540×960 edit of those five images with a restrained camera push, burnt-in captions and local Windows synthesized narration. captions.vtt provides optional selectable captions. It is a motion edit of still photography, not generated live-action footage. poster.jpg is its first frame.

scripts/build-demo-video.py reproduces the edit with imageio-ffmpeg, using .work/voice.wav and .work/arial.ttf from the local Windows build environment. Original generated image retained in Codex generated_images.

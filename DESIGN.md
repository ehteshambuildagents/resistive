# Design
A continuous dark product film for performance marketers. Preserve Geist and Resistive's mark. Photographic shots of the same unbranded device act as persistent objects. Evidence is recognizable advertising material: 9:16 ad frames, timed creative events read off the ad itself, performance observations shown beside the creative that produced them, and at most two short customer questions in a supporting role. Not quotations as the reasoning layer, not format tallies, not abstract nodes. One dominant narrative at a time. Blue denotes Resistive acting: the selected test, the moment under inspection, the more efficient ad. Warm denotes customer language. Sample figures are labelled illustrative in frame. Story: understand, decide, create, test, learn. Sample assets and numbers are illustrative.
One sticky stage, one GSAP timeline, one ScrollTrigger, one Lenis ticker; transform and opacity animation only. Normal wheel distance with modest easing. Mobile has a vertical evidence column and three prominent shots. Waitlist outside the stage.
